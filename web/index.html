How it will be sung

Dots mark syllable breaks. English is irregular: where a split is wrong, write the hyphens yourself (or-ange), put * before a stressed syllable (*to-day) and hold a syllable over 2 to 4 notes with ~ or ~3. Text you hyphenate is left as written.

Seed, voice and playback

Rendering is deterministic: the same song, seed and voice give the same recording here. It is not bit-identical to the native build (different math library, no flush-to-zero); the difference is 132 dB below the signal. The singer is synthetic. Your edits are kept in this browser.
