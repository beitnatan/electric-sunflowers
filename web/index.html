Player

Write the words as you would say them. One line per sung line; a blank line or a header such as [chorus] starts a section. A header alone repeats the earlier section of that type. The engine composes the melody and the band; key, tempo, voice and band are on the Arrangement layer.

How the words are split

Words are split into syllables by a rule of thumb, and the engine stresses the first syllable of each long word and each content word. English is irregular: where it is wrong, write the hyphens yourself (or-ange), mark stress with * before a syllable (*to-day) and hold a syllable over 2 to 4 notes with ~ or ~3. Text you hyphenate is left as written. The Arrangement layer shows the result per line.

How the song is played: key, tempo, the band, the singers, then each section with its chords, energy and melody hints. Anything left on auto is decided by the engine, the same way every time for a given seed.

Rendering is deterministic: the same song, seed and voice give the same recording here. It is not bit-identical to the native build (different math library, no flush-to-zero); the difference is 132 dB below the signal. Your edits are kept in this browser.
